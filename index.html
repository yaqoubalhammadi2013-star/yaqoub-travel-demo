GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Here's how searching and booking with YAQOUB TRAVEL works.

Still have a question? →
Are flight and hotel prices live on this website?+

This website currently has no connected live travel API. Search forms open Google Flights or Booking.com, where you can see current availability and provider-supplied prices. We never show sample fares as if they were live.

How do I book a trip?+

Choose your journey or stay and continue to the airline or travel provider. They handle the booking, payment, changes and customer support. YAQOUB TRAVEL does not collect card details or issue booking confirmations.

Why don't you show discount prices?+

We only show a saving when a booking provider supplies a verifiable original price and discounted price. No verified offer feed is connected yet, so our destination picks are inspiration—not claimed discounts.

Can I compare Abu Dhabi and Dubai departures?+

Yes. Choose AUH (Abu Dhabi) or DXB (Dubai) in the flight search. The provider search opens with your selected origin, where you can review available airlines, schedules, baggage terms and current prices.

Are the hotel photos and ratings live listings?+

Photos on our inspiration pages illustrate destinations and stays. They are not representations of a specific bookable hotel. Search Booking.com for actual property listings, current photos, verified guest ratings and rates.

Can I change or cancel my reservation here?+

Reservations are made with the provider. Please contact the airline, hotel or booking platform directly and check its fare rules and cancellation terms before you book.
